Which bin does this go in?

Upload a photo of an item and a convolutional network trained on the Waste Segregation dataset sorts it into one of four disposal categories.

Hazardous
Batteries, chemicals, e-waste — needs special handling
Non-Recyclable
Mixed or soiled materials headed for landfill
Organic
Food scraps and plant matter, compostable
Recyclable
Clean paper, glass, metal and plastic
